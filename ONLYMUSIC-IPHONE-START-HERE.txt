OnlyMusic for iPhone - iOS 18 and newer

Native iPhone adaptation of BitChord for Apple, with OnlyMusic branding,
quiet first-launch defaults and app-process widget audio intents.

Upstream: https://github.com/bagumamartin/BitChord
Base revision: 898d8ed746b1bfdeb26a4c2d24484844241ed990
License: GPL-3.0; upstream and third-party notices are retained.

Build: Actions -> Build OnlyMusic iPhone -> Run workflow.
The manual workflow builds on a standard GitHub-hosted macOS runner and
uploads an unsigned OnlyMusic.ipa. It does not publish releases or use Apple
credentials. Download the artifact and sign/install with your own Apple ID.
Windows installation guide:
https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-classic/how-to-install-altstore-windows

The deployment floor is iOS 18; the source currently needs an iOS 27 SDK.
The newer SDK does not change the minimum iOS version of the packaged app.

This is an early adaptation, not a verified release. Native screens differ
from Android OnlyMusic. Android databases and recent mix snapshots are not
imported. Sign in again for your online YouTube Music account.
The widget uses the Apple base's now-playing view, not Android's custom
recent-mix layout. Widget artwork and titles require an App Group supported
by your signing setup; without it a generic playback button is shown.
Google sign-in still uses Google's webpage, not another app's saved account.
Live streaming, login and background audio must be checked on an iPhone.
